# Mohamed Omar — Portfolio

A responsive bilingual portfolio featuring Mohamed's supplied portrait and certificates, and three live websites.

## Open

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation or build step is needed. Google Fonts load when the browser is online; system fonts are used otherwise.

## Included

- English and Arabic with right-to-left layout and remembered language choice
- Hero section with a terminal-style profile card
- Supplied portrait in the hero and About sections
- Three project cards with local screenshots and live preview links: EXTRA TOP, Fruit Shop, and REVO Coffee
- Education: El Saleheya El Gadida University, Faculty of Computers and Information, CS & AI, third year
- Project, study, certificate, and contact sections
- Four supplied certificate scans in a horizontal, responsive carousel with click-to-enlarge viewing
- Supplied images organized under `assets/profile/`, `assets/certificates/`, and `assets/projects/`
- Simple slide navigation and subtle entrance motion for certificate cards
- The ITI 120-hour course breakdown, grouped by web, Python/API, and AI/data topics
- Contact panel and footer with Facebook and Instagram links
- Inline SVG platform logos
- Responsive layout, scroll reveal, mobile navigation, and reduced-motion support
